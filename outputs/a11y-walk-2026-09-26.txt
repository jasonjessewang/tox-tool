ACCESSIBILITY SPOT CHECK ACROSS EVERY SCREEN (final build, 2026-09-26)

Method
  scripts/a11y-audit.js run on each screen by scripts/a11y-walk.js, in the web build at 375 x 812 (a phone), with the storage of a
  simulated person with history loaded (Priya, after 84 days) and "Straight there" chosen under About you. Screens were reached by
  tapping. The same walk was repeated at 320 x 640 (the width WCAG uses for reflow).
  Each line: headings (how many, how many h1, how many skipped levels) . controls without an accessible name out of all controls .
  controls sharing a name . targets under 24 x 24 px . text under 4.5:1 contrast (3:1 for large text) . text under 11 px .
  radios / checkboxes / tabs whose state is not exposed to assistive technology. Horizontal overflow is flagged when it occurs;
  it did not occur on any screen at either width.

Not reached by the walk
  Tutorial quests, with history (the person had finished the starter quests, so there was no "Show me" step to open it from): it was
  audited in the fresh-install state instead (above).

What a spot check cannot tell
  Whether the reading order and focus after a screen change make sense; how live regions sound; whether the labels read well aloud;
  dynamic type and switch control on a device; the camera scan. Keyboard focus was checked visually with the Tab key (a visible focus
  ring on the Learn tabs). Someone should go through the app once with VoiceOver or TalkBack.
