Plain-language background on everything the fusion engine watches for, with real peer-reviewed sources you can check yourself — not just a claim to take on faith. Concern level is a coarse 1–3 heuristic, not a dose-adjusted risk rating; see the note on every page.

How to read "concern level"

These scores reflect how consistently a substance shows up in public-health/ regulatory guidance as worth managing — they do not account for dose. The amount in a typical product or serving is almost always far below what's studied in the underlying research. GHS hazard statements below (when present) describe the pure, concentrated substance as handled industrially — not the diluted trace amount in a consumer product.
